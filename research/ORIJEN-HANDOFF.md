## Conflicts / generation notes

- Current ORIJEN USA lists the large bags for Original, Six Fish, Regional Red, Puppy, Puppy Large, Senior and Tundra as **23.5 lb**. Many legacy retailer records still call the same UPCs **25 lb**. Treat this as packaging-size relabeling under the same UPC, not a second product.
- Current US Tundra dry dog food lists only **4.5 lb and 23.5 lb**. A 13 lb Tundra appears in older retailer data; do not treat it as current without current ORIJEN evidence.
- `064992` is strongly supported as an ORIJEN/Champion package prefix from the 20 independently bound records above. Prefix alone must not be used to distinguish ORIJEN from ACANA.
- The ORIJEN site has some stale/non-US pages under `en-US` URLs (for example FEDIAF/Canada-style pages). Only pages carrying the current US formula/AAFCO statements and US package sizes are used here.
