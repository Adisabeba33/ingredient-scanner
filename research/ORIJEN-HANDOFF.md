## US versus Canada

US records in these two batches are built from current ORIJEN USA product pages plus US retailer/distributor package evidence. The retained evidence does **not** establish a general rule that US and Canadian ORIJEN share the same UPCs; treat market identity as unresolved per product unless the package itself is proven US-market.

## GS1 / company-prefix answer

`064992` is strongly established as a Champion/ORIJEN package prefix across the independently bound records in these batches. It is **not a brand discriminator**: Champion makes both ORIJEN and ACANA, so a `064992…` code cannot by itself prove the pack is ORIJEN. No new prefix should be registered from this handoff alone; the seeding pass owns `data/gs1-prefixes.ts`.

## ACANA boundary / named near-misses

No **named** ACANA near-miss was retained in the Batch 1/2 evidence trail. That is a provenance gap and must not be repaired by inventing product names from memory after the fact. The operative rule remains: a package/page must explicitly identify **ORIJEN**; “Champion Petfoods”, “Biologically Appropriate”, a shared kitchen, or the `064992` prefix is insufficient because the same maker and prefix family can also identify ACANA. Future batches should record every rejected ACANA product by exact name when encountered.

## Recalls — explicit negative

**No U.S. ORIJEN recall was found in the research trail used for this campaign.** Do not create an ORIJEN row in `data/recalls.ts` from any of the following, because none is a recall:

1. FDA's June 2019 DCM investigation/update named ORIJEN among brands reported in DCM case reports. It was an investigation/report count, not a causation finding and not a recall.
2. Consumer class actions over grain-free/legume-heavy formulas or marketing claims are litigation, not recalls.
3. The 2008 Australian irradiation event involved food treated for Australian import requirements and is not a U.S. recall.
