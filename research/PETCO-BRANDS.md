# Petco dog & cat food brand survey

Checked: **2026-09-21**

## Scope and method

Primary Petco sources:

- All Brands: https://www.petco.com/shop/ShopAllBrandsView
- Dry dog food: https://www.petco.com/category/dog/dog-food/dry-dog-food
- Wet dog food: https://www.petco.com/category/dog/dog-food/wet-dog-food
- Dry cat food: https://www.petco.com/category/cat/cat-food/dry-cat-food
- Wet cat food: https://www.petco.com/category/cat/cat-food/wet-cat-food

This survey is **food only**. Treats, chews, supplements, broths/toppers sold only as complements, litter, grooming, bowls, apparel and other supplies are excluded.

Petco's category facets are not clean food truth. The four target facets currently expose **54 dry-dog + 52 wet-dog + 37 dry-cat + 48 wet-cat brand labels**, forming **89 unique candidate labels**. Each candidate was then checked against its Petco brand/product pages. **13 candidate labels were rejected as non-food/false-facet cases**, leaving **76 qualifying food brands**.

`Products` means qualifying Petco food listings I could establish from the target pages/brand pages on the checked date. When Petco mixes meals with toppers/treats or its page counts conflict, the cell says `не установлено точно` (or a proven lower bound) rather than guessing. Counts are a survey snapshot, not a promise of local-store stock.
